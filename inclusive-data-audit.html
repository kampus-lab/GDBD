Inclusive Data Audit

A framework for auditing whose data is represented — and whose is missing — across collection, modeling, and product decisions. Work through each section with your team; check items off as you confirm them.

0 of 0 checked 0%

1. Data Collection

before you collect

Complete before collecting any new data, or before reusing an existing dataset.

  • Who are we assuming our users are? (age, language, device, ability, income, geography)
  • Who does our collection method exclude by default?
  • What happens to someone who doesn't fit our existing categories?
  • Who collected any data we're reusing, and for what original purpose?

2. Representation

on the dataset itself

Complete once the dataset exists, before it's used to train or ship anything.

3. Model / Metric

before shipping

Complete before shipping any feature, model, or scoring system.

4. Labor & Process

who did the invisible work

Complete when data has been labeled, annotated, or otherwise produced by human labor.

5. Governance

ongoing practice

Standing practices, not one-time checks.

Quick version — three questions before every launch

  1. Who's in our data, and who's structurally left out?
  2. If we broke our top metric down by subgroup, would the story change?
  3. Who would be hurt first if this dataset's gaps caused an error — and would we find out?
